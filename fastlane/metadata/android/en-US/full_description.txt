Caster turns your phone into a small media server for your home network. Start sharing, then watch your videos, photos and music on a TV or any web browser on the same Wi-Fi. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.

Features:
• One-tap sharing from the app, a quick-settings tile, a home-screen widget or a shortcut
• Find your phone from a TV browser: it announces itself as caster.local
• Pairing: approve each new device on your phone by matching codes
• Optional PIN lock for the app
• Remembers where you stopped watching and finds subtitles automatically
• Watch history and a diagnostics screen
• Built-in browser that detects video on web pages
• Remote control for Android TV, Fire TV and WebSocket TVs, with Wake-on-LAN and voice input
• Keeps sharing with the screen off (asks to be excluded from battery optimisation)

Permissions: network access, media access (to share your files), microphone (voice input for the TV remote), notifications and start at boot.

No ads. No tracking.
